The DFG benchmark of Schäfer & Turek (1996): a cylinder of diameter D = 0.1 m at (0.2, 0.2) m in a 2.2 m × 0.41 m channel with a parabolic inflow, ν = 10−3 m²/s, ρ = 1 kg/m³. Seventeen research groups submitted results; the paper's tables end with intervals in which the authors estimated the exact values to lie. More precise values were published later by the same group and are shown as a second reference row.

Set-up here: uniform grid with the stated number of cells per diameter (the fluid region is 22n × 4.1n cells), interpolated bounce-back on the cylinder, velocity bounce-back inlet, constant-pressure outlet that absorbs plane sound waves, forces by momentum exchange, pressure difference from densities extrapolated quadratically to the front and rear points of the cylinder. Coefficients are 2F / (ρ U² D) with U the mean inflow velocity. The last row of each table repeats the 20-cell run at half the lattice velocity (half the Mach number, twice the number of steps) to show the size of the compressibility error.
