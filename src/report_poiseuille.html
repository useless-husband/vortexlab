Flow between two parallel plates driven by a uniform force has the exact parabolic profile u(y) = g y (H − y) / 2ν. The channel is periodic along the flow, so the only error sources are the collision model and the wall rule. Resolution is doubled repeatedly with the relaxation time fixed (τ = 0.8) and the velocity scaled like 1/H.

Three things are checked: the error falls with the square of the resolution (observed order 2); with the two-relaxation-time model and Λ = 3/16 the halfway bounce-back wall is exactly where it should be and the parabola is reproduced to rounding error, as theory predicts (Ginzburg, Verhaeghe & d'Humières 2008); and with the walls placed a quarter or four fifths of a cell beyond the last fluid row, the interpolated bounce-back rule keeps second order. The last column is the drag on the two walls from the momentum-exchange force evaluation divided by the total driving force: it must be 1.
