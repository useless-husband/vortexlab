Reading these tables. The 1996 intervals are narrow (±0.2 % for the steady drag, ±0.3 % for the maximum drag), narrower than what most of the original participants achieved, and one of them does not contain the later reference value: the maximum lift of case 2D-2 from the later high-resolution computation is below the 1996 lower bound. Values that are outside an interval are marked as such, with the distance; they were not tuned.

Run at 10 cells per diameter, case 2D-2 diverges (τ = 0.515 with velocity gradients the grid cannot represent); the program reports that instead of numbers, and the case is left out of the table.
